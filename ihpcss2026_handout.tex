\begin{frame}{What is the IHPCSS?}
\phantomsection\label{what-is-the-ihpcss}
\pause

\begin{itemize}
\item
  The International High Performance Computing Summer School is a one-week, intensive, in-person training event held annually since 2010 at locations around the world.

  \pause
\item
  International partners like Access (US), EuroHPC (EU), RIKEN (Japan), Pawsey (Australia), and CHPC (South Africa) select students with strong potential in HPC.

  \pause
\item
  Partners fund student participation, instructors, and staff.

  \pause
\item
  The event includes a unique mentoring component to help students build careers in HPC.

  \pause
\item
  Compute Canada was a partner from 2014--2017, SciNet from 2018--2023, and the Alliance from 2025 onward.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{NTCC IHPCSS Working Group}
\phantomsection\label{ntcc-ihpcss-working-group}
\pause

\vspace{-3mm}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  Being a partner involves:

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    attending international organizing committee meetings
  \item
    contributing to program development
  \item
    running the Canadian selection process
  \item
    inviting selected students
  \item
    supporting them during the event
  \end{itemize}

  \pause
\item
  The Alliance delegated these tasks to the National Training Coordination Council (NTCC), which started a working group (WG).

  \pause
\item
  The Alliance required a pilot funding proposal for the event, which the WG prepared. (It helped that the Alliance Training Coordinator is part of the WG).

  \pause
\item
  Finance and expense conditions were handled by the Alliance in 2025, and by SciNet in 2026 (requiring another proposal to the Alliance.)
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Evolving content}
\phantomsection\label{evolving-content}
\begin{itemize}
\item
  The main focus remains large-scale HPC.

  \pause
\item
  Technical content is reviewed annually.

  \pause
\item
  The IHPCSS has changed significantly since 2010:

  \pause

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    GPU programming is now a standard component.
  \item
    Big data/machine learning/AI component continues to grow.
  \item
    There's even an Python for HPC session.
  \item
    But, intentionally, it is not becoming an AI conference.
  \item
    The mentoring component is unique and well-established.
  \end{itemize}

  \pause
\item
  Every year, substantial effort goes into collecting feedback, assessing the skill landscape and updating the technical content.
\end{itemize}

\pause

\small

Scott Callaghan et al.~2025. Fifteen Years of International HPC Summer School. In PEARC '25, ACM, New York, NY, USA, Article 17, 1--8. \textbf{\alert{\url{https://doi.org/10.1145/3708035.3736011}}}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Impact}
\phantomsection\label{impact}
\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textasciitilde92\% found that \textbf{\alert{participants from other countries}} enhanced their learning.

  \pause
\item
  \textasciitilde97\% rated the \textbf{\alert{experience successful}}.

  \pause
\item
  The \textbf{\alert{structured mentoring program}} was identified as one of the most unique and valuable aspects.

  \pause
\item
  The 10 students from Canada get to \textbf{\alert{network}} with \textasciitilde70 peers and \textasciitilde30 international experts, including returning mentors.

  \pause
\item
  The 4 Canada \textbf{\alert{staff members}} get to \textbf{\alert{network}} with \textasciitilde30 international peers.

  \pause
\item
  The \textbf{\alert{Alliance/Canada}} gains \textbf{\alert{exposure}} to \textasciitilde30 international HPC peers.

  \pause
\item
  \textbf{\alert{100}} students have been sent from Canada over the last 12 years, many moving into leading roles in \textbf{\alert{academia}}, \textbf{\alert{government}}, and \textbf{\alert{industry}}.
\end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{Lessons Learned}
\phantomsection\label{lessons-learned}
\pause

\begin{itemize}
\item
  Budgets need healthy margins for rising costs and uncertain participant origins.

  \pause
\item
  VISAs remain unpredictable; in 2025, two students could not attend because of VISA appointment delays.

  \pause
\item
  The working group only needs frequent meetings during a few key periods.

  \pause
\item
  We need more reviewers (or less advertising\ldots).

  \pause
\item
  Coordinating finances across two organizations is slow and painful;\\
  best avoided.
\end{itemize}
